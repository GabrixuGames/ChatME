No. Pixel-\/based constraints \href{https://github.com/bvaughn/react-resizable-panels/pull/176}{\texttt{added significant complexity}} to the initialization and validation logic and so I\textquotesingle{}ve decided not to support them. \hypertarget{dir_4663926ab448c02f1eaed0c111f2baae_autotoc_md5324}{}\doxysubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{Can panel sizes be persistent?}{Can panel sizes be persistent?}}\label{dir_4663926ab448c02f1eaed0c111f2baae_autotoc_md5324}
Yes. Panel groups with an {\ttfamily auto\+Save\+Id} prop will automatically save and restore their layouts on mount.\hypertarget{dir_4663926ab448c02f1eaed0c111f2baae_autotoc_md5325}{}\doxysubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{How can I use persistent layouts with SSR?}{How can I use persistent layouts with SSR?}}\label{dir_4663926ab448c02f1eaed0c111f2baae_autotoc_md5325}
