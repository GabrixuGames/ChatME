By default the wrap is soft, meaning long words may extend past the column width. Setting this to {\ttfamily true} will make it hard wrap at the column width.\hypertarget{dir_c3ea02277849ae2d132d2b6617209e89_autotoc_md7900}{}\doxysubsubsubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{word\+Wrap}{word\+Wrap}}\label{dir_c3ea02277849ae2d132d2b6617209e89_autotoc_md7900}

By default, an attempt is made to split words at spaces, ensuring that they don\textquotesingle{}t extend past the configured columns. If word\+Wrap is {\ttfamily false}, each column will instead be completely filled splitting words as necessary.\hypertarget{dir_c3ea02277849ae2d132d2b6617209e89_autotoc_md7901}{}\doxysubsubsubsubsection{\texorpdfstring{trim}{trim}}\label{dir_c3ea02277849ae2d132d2b6617209e89_autotoc_md7901}
